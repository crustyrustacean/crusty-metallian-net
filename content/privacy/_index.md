I’m Jeffery D. Mitchell, and I run The Crusty Metallian, a personal music blog. This page explains how the site handles visitor information.

## Hosting and security

Cloudflare hosts and protects the site. It processes connection information such as your IP address, requested URLs, and browser details. Its security features may set cookies. See [Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Visitor statistics

I use Plausible Analytics to see which pages people read and how they find the blog. Plausible reports aggregate statistics and does not use analytics cookies or persistent visitor identifiers. See [Plausible’s Data Policy](https://plausible.io/data-policy).

## Widgets and external links

Your browser loads the Datastar script from jsDelivr and contacts Musicfeed to display the current rotation and artist stats. Those requests include ordinary connection information such as your IP address. External sites, including Metallian Photos, handle visitor information under their own policies.
